autorenew Returns Policy

We offer a 30-day free returns policy on all orders. Items must be returned in their original condition — unworn, unwashed, with all tags attached.

To initiate a return, contact our customer service team at returns@ellore.de with your order number. We will send you a prepaid DHL return label within 24 hours.

Refunds are processed within 5–7 business days of receiving the returned item. The refund will be credited to your original payment method.

inventory_2 Order Processing

Orders placed before 14:00 CET Monday–Friday are dispatched the same day. Orders placed after 14:00 or on weekends are dispatched the next business day.

Once your order is dispatched, you will receive a tracking number via email. Orders are shipped from our Berlin warehouse via DHL.

receipt Customs & Duties

All prices displayed on ELLORE include German VAT (19%). For orders shipped outside the EU, customs duties and import taxes may apply. These are the responsibility of the recipient and are not included in the shipping cost.
